2.5.3.2  Convolutional Neural Networks
In CNNs, the convolution operation can be viewed as a series of small, local linear transformations. 
A convolutional filter (or kernel) slides over different local regions of the input image, applying a 
linear transformation to produce a feature map. By stacking multiple convolution layers, CNNs 
detect complex patterns such as edges, textures, and shapes.
