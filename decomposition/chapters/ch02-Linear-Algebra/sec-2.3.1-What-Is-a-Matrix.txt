2.3.1  What Is a Matrix?
A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers, symbols, or expressions arranged in rows and columns. 
Matrices are denoted by capital letters (e.g., A, B, C) and are often used in systems of linear 
equations, computer graphics, statistics, and many other areas.
