(b)	 Dropout: Dropout is a regularization technique used to prevent overfitting by randomly 
“dropping out” or turning off neurons during each forward and backward pass with a prob-
ability p. During training, at each iteration, a fraction of neurons (typically p =​ 0.5) are set to 
zero, which forces the network to rely on multiple pathways for learning. This helps make the 
network more robust and prevents neurons from co-​adapting too strongly to specific features.

The network then continues training with these modified activations. At test time, dropout 
is turned off, but the output is scaled by a factor of 1 − p to account for the dropped neurons

dropout are essential techniques for preventing overfitting and ensuring that NNs generalize 
well to unseen data.
