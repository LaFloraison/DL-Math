2.5.2.1  Scaling
Scaling involves multiplying vectors by a scalar, stretching or shrinking them but not changing their 
direction unless the scalar is negative. A vector can be scaled using a diagonal matrix where each 
diagonal entry corresponds to a scaling factor along each dimension.
