11.2  THEORY OF DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS AND DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS
Dynamical systems and differential equations help us understand how things change, grow, or 
decrease over time, both in nature and in human-​made systems. These math ideas let us study and 
predict how complex systems behave as they develop. By learning about them, we can see the 
patterns that shape the world around us and use these principles in different areas, including tech-
nology and neural networks.
