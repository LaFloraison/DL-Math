11.2.1  Dynamical Systems
A dynamical system is a way to describe how something changes over time using a set of rules. 
These systems are used in many areas of science and engineering to track things like how planets 
move or how populations grow. The strength of dynamical systems is their ability to show how 
different states change, making them useful for understanding and predicting behavior over time. In 
math, they are shown by maps that illustrate how one state changes to another, allowing us to follow 
paths and predict future states in a certain space.
