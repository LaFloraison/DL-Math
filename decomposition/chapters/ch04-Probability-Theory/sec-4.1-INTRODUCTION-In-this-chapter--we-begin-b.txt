4.1  INTRODUCTION
In this chapter, we begin by exploring the fundamental role of probability distributions in 
understanding and modeling the randomness inherent in real-​world data. From the discrete sim-
plicity of a dice roll to the continuous variations in financial markets, probability distributions help 
us frame our predictive models in terms that align closely with observed phenomena. As we navi-
gate this chapter, we introduce you to various probability distributions tailored to specific data and 
analysis requirements. We also discuss how these distributions interact with neural network (NN) 
architectures, particularly Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs), to provide a robust framework for 
handling uncertainty and making informed predictions.
