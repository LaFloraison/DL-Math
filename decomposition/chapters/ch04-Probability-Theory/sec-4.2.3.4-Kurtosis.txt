4.2.3.5  Mode
The mode is the value that appears most frequently in the distribution. In a unimodal distribu-
tion (like the normal distribution), the mode coincides with the mean. However, for multimodal 
distributions, there may be more than one mode. The mode is useful for understanding the most fre-
quent outcomes in a distribution, particularly for discrete data or skewed distributions. In a normal 
distribution, the mode is equal to the mean (μ =​ 50), and the most frequent value is 50. In a right-​
skewed distribution, the mode would be less than the mean and closer to the peak of the distribution.
