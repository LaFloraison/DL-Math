Techniques like bootstrapping estimate properties of estimators by sampling from an approximating 
distribution. These methods are commonly used to assess the variability or stability of statistical 
models, providing insights into the accuracy and reliability of the model’s predictions by repeatedly 
sampling with replacement from the dataset to create numerous simulated samples. For each boot-

4.3.13  Sampling Methods and Overfitting/​Underfitting
Sampling methods are pivotal in how well a statistical model can learn and generalize from data. 
Improper sampling can lead to overfitting and underfitting, each affecting model performance 
significantly.
