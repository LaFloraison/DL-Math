5.6.4  Momentum
Momentum is an optimization technique used to accelerate gradient descent by considering past 
gradients in the update process. This approach helps gradient descent algorithms converge faster 
and reduce oscillations, especially in cases where the objective function’s surface has features of 
different scales or saddle points. The key idea behind momentum is to maintain a velocity vector 
that accumulates the gradient of the loss function over time. This accumulated gradient is used to 
update the model parameters, resulting in faster convergence and smoother trajectories. The velocity 
vector is updated using a combination of the current gradient and the previous velocity. The updated 
equations for gradient descent with momentum are as follows:
