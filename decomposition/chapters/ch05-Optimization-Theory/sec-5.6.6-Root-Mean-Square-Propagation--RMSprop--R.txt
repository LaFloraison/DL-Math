5.6.6  Root Mean Square Propagation (RMSprop)
RMSprop is an optimization algorithm designed to address the issue of diminishing learning rates in 
Adagrad. By introducing a decay factor, RMSprop ensures that the learning rate does not decrease 
too aggressively, making it more suitable for training deep networks. RMSprop modifies Adagrad by 
maintaining a moving average of the squared gradients instead of accumulating all the past squared 
gradients. This moving average helps to prevent the learning rates from becoming excessively small, 
allowing the optimization process to continue learning effectively over time. The update rule for 
RMSprop is as follows:

making it more suitable for training deep neural networks. The controlled adjustment of learning
