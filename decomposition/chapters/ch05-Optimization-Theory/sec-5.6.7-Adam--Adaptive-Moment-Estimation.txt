5.6.7  Adam (Adaptive Moment Estimation)
Adam is an optimization algorithm that combines the benefits of both Adagrad and RMSprop. 
It leverages the advantages of adaptive learning rates and incorporates momentum to accelerate 
convergence and stabilize updates. Adam maintains individual learning rates for each param-
eter by computing the first and second moments of the gradients. The first moment is the mean 
of the gradients, and the second is the gradients’ uncentered variance. These moments are used 
to adapt the learning rates for each parameter dynamically. The updated rules for Adam are as
