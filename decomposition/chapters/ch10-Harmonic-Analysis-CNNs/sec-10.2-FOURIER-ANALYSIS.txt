10.2.1   f undamental S of fourier  analy SiS
The strength of Fourier analysis is that it can break any complex signal into simple sine and cosine 
waves. This separation lets us look closely at the signal’s different frequencies. Understanding these 
frequencies is important in many areas, from engineering to science. It helps us design filters, study 
waveforms, and process data more effectively.
