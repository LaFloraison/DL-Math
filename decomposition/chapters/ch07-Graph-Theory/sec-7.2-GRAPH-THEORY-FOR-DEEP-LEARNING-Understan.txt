7.2  GRAPH THEORY FOR DEEP LEARNING
Understanding the basic types of graphs and their properties is fundamental for modeling and 
solving problems in various domains, including computer science, biology, social sciences, 
and more. Graphs provide a useful and powerful way to represent and analyze the relationships and 
structures within data.
7.2.1  Graph
A graph is a fundamental mathematical structure consisting of a set of nodes (vertices) and edges. 
The key components of the graph are nodes and edges.
