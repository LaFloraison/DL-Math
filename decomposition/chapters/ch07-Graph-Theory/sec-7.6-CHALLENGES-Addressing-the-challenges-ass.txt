7.6  CHALLENGES
Addressing the challenges associated with GCNs is crucial for their broader adoption and effect-
iveness in real-​world applications. Researchers are actively developing techniques to improve 
scalability, handle dynamic graphs, and manage heterogeneous graphs more effectively. These 
advancements aim to make GCNs more robust, efficient, and capable of dealing with the complex-
ities of real-​world graph data. In this section, we discuss some of these challenges.
